Featured Project

Active Cell Balancing of Lithium-Ion Batteries for an EV

IIPE Visakhapatnam · 8 Dec 2025 – 7 Apr 2026 · B.Tech project report. Guide: Dr. D. Kishore, Assistant Professor, Dept. of E&I Engineering. Teammate: K. Devi Prasad.

Overview

Cells in a pack drift apart in State of Charge (SOC). Without balancing, the weakest or most-discharged cell limits usable pack capacity and consistency. Active balancing moves energy between cells instead of dissipating it.

Objectives

Investigate active cell balancing, BMS concepts, SOC/SOH estimation and energy transfer between cells.

Technical approach

MATLAB/Simulink battery modeling and SOC monitoring. The report discusses switched-capacitor and inductor-based DC–DC balancing, buck–boost converter concepts, closed-loop control, PI, PWM and switching logic. These are methods studied and modeled in simulation, not a hardware-ready integrated system.

System architecture (conceptual representation)

Battery pack / cell models→Voltage & current measurement→SOC information→Cell selection & balancing control→Converter switching logic→Energy transfer between cells→Monitoring & evaluation

Results and engineering analysis

Distribution comparison

Initial SOC values span roughly 40–90%. The plotted final distribution spans roughly 65–75%, which suggests the intended balancing outcome.

Time-based simulation

The SOC-imbalance curve stays nearly constant at about a 50% spread, and individual SOC traces change little over the 60-minute run. Expected dynamic balancing is not consistently demonstrated.

Engineering Challenges & Next Steps

The balancing algorithm is not claimed as fully validated. The report points to these areas for further investigation:

  • SOC update equations
  • Converter current and battery model interaction
  • Energy-transfer implementation
  • Dynamic simulation and validation

Learning: control logic, battery state updates, converter behavior and measurable simulation results must be connected end to end, otherwise a plausible-looking result can hide a model gap.
